Frequently Asked Questions

▶ How does the Solar Smart Bench work?

Solar panels collect sunlight and convert it into electrical energy stored in the battery system.

▶ Can I charge my devices at night?

Yes, stored battery energy allows users to charge devices after sunset.

▶ What devices can be charged?

The bench supports charging smartphones, tablets, laptops and USB supported devices.

▶ Is the bench weather resistant?

The bench is designed using durable materials suitable for outdoor environments.

▶ Where can the Solar Smart Bench be installed?

It can be installed in universities, parks, streets, transportation areas and smart cities.
